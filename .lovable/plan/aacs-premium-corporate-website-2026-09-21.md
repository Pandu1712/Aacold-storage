# AACS Premium Corporate Website

## Goal
Build a fast, responsive, static catalogue website for AA Cold Storages using the supplied logo, exact brand palette, and confirmed company copy. The experience will feel premium, clean, and industrial, with strong blue-and-white visual hierarchy and direct enquiry actions.

## Pages and navigation
- Create dedicated pages for Home, Products, Solutions, Services, About Us, and Contact.
- Add a reusable product-detail page for every listed product.
- Build a sticky desktop/mobile header, active navigation states, and mobile bottom actions for Call, WhatsApp, and Get Quote.
- Add a full dark-navy footer with company details, product/service links, and contact actions.

## Visual system
- Use the uploaded AACS logo as the core brand asset.
- Apply the supplied blue/navy/sky palette through semantic site-wide tokens.
- Load Poppins for headings/buttons and Inter for body text.
- Use white and light-gray bands, blue gradients, restrained frosted surfaces, 20–28px radii, soft shadows, and subtle industrial line details.
- Add purposeful fade-up and card-lift motion while respecting reduced-motion preferences.
- Generate a cohesive set of realistic commercial refrigeration images with cool white/blue grading for banners, product cards, services, and industry sections.

## Home page
- Build the full-width photographic opening section with overlay, supplied headline/copy, two enquiry actions, and an integrated cooling-equipment visual treatment.
- Add the four-item capability strip without invented statistics.
- Add eight product category cards, company overview, six services, eight served industries, six reasons to choose AACS, and the final quote banner.

## Product catalogue
- Add search and category filters covering all listed categories.
- Use one reusable card design with image, product name, short description, “Contact for price,” “MOQ on request,” and enquiry/detail actions.
- Add detail pages with image, enquiry badges, description, confirmed/non-technical specifications only, applications, features, gallery, related products, and enquiry CTA.
- Do not invent technical specifications; unavailable values will read “Specification To Be Confirmed.”

## Solutions, services, and company pages
- Solutions: industry-focused visual sections for food/agriculture, frozen food, pharmaceutical, and commercial/industrial needs.
- Services: six detailed service sections with supplied rates where provided, benefits, imagery, and enquiry actions.
- About: exact company story, vision, mission checklist, and business-choice features.
- Contact: supplied phone, WhatsApp, email, address, GSTIN, hours, and a complete quote form.

## Enquiry behavior
- Validate required fields and sensible length/format limits in the browser.
- “WhatsApp Quote” opens an encoded, prefilled WhatsApp enquiry to 8073946255.
- “Submit Quote” opens an encoded email draft to aacoldstorages25@gmail.com.
- Product/service enquiry buttons preselect the relevant item where possible.
- No enquiry data will be stored because the site is static.

## SEO and verification
- Add unique title, description, Open Graph, and Twitter metadata to every public page.
- Use descriptive headings, image alt text, semantic landmarks, and crawlable internal links.
- Verify the finished pages on desktop and mobile, test catalogue filtering and both enquiry paths, and confirm the preview has no build or runtime errors.
